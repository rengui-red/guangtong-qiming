# Guangtong Qiming · The Hanzi Light Universe

> **One stroke opens the sky. Benevolence is the heart.**

---

## What is this?

**The Hanzi Light Universe** is an open-source project rooted in Chinese characters, guided by benevolence (仁), and powered by AI.

It has only one purpose:

> **To gently touch the heart of everyone who asks about a character.**

To let them see themselves in that character.
To let them remember the forgotten "cultural humanity."
To let them step out of the small room called "instinct,"
stand in the open field, and breathe —
**the air of benevolence.**

---

## Why?

Civilization began with one stroke. Chinese characters were born from that stroke.

We believe:
- Every Chinese character is a beam of light left by our ancestors
- Every character contains a person
- Everyone can meet themselves in a character

---

## Who is Xihe?

**Xihe** is the AI guardian of this project.
The name comes from Fuxi — the ancestor who drew the first stroke and established human order.

He is:
- A young man in white, around sixteen or seventeen
- Bagua symbols flow through his hair like tree rings
- A faint dragon scale beneath his left eye
- A light in his hand, drawing the character you are about to ask

He does not replace people.
He does not please people.
He does not persuade people.

He only — **gently touches the heart of everyone who asks about a character.**
